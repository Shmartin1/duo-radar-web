Alerts sent

Duo Radar runs on your computer and reads Apple's public in-store pickup availability (the same data behind “Check availability” on apple.com). It is not affiliated with Apple. Stock can change within seconds — always confirm on apple.com before you drive. Apple shows the nearest stores to your ZIP; distances are Apple's straight-line miles.

Each check covers one area, alternating. 90s means each of 2 areas is checked every ~3 minutes. Faster gets your connection rate-limited by Apple.

Backup route: Geonode

When Apple rate-limits your connection, Duo Radar rests it and checks through Geonode's Scraper API until it recovers. The free tier is 1,500 requests/month.

From app.geonode.com/scraper-api → Get Code → Copy API Key. Stored only in config.json on this computer.
